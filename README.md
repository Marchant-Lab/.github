# Marchant Lab

The Marchant Lab at the Universidad Industrial de Santander (UIS) integrates physiology, genomics, and transcriptomics. Our research bridges basic and applied science, leveraging molecular ecology to address critical challenges in biodiversity conservation, plant-pathogen interactions, and sustainable agricultural biotechnology.

We are based in the **Escuela de Biología** at **UIS** in Bucaramanga, Santander, Colombia.

---

## Core Research Axes

### 1. Agricultural Biotechnology & Phytopathology
In close collaboration with institutions like AGROSAVIA, we investigate the occurrence, molecular characterization, and genetic variability of plant pathogens and agricultural pests to promote sustainable farming practices.
* **Plant-Parasitic Nematodes:** Investigating the genetic variability and occurrence of cyst nematodes (*Globodera rostochiensis* and *Globodera spp.*) impacting potato crops.
* **Fungal Pathogens:** Diagnosing and managing descending dieback (*Lasiodiplodia spp.*) in avocado crops, alongside tracking native *Fusarium oxysporum* strains.
* **Soil Arthropods:** Conducting morphological and molecular identification of crop-associated symphylans, including newly described species *Hanseniella chocoita* and *Hanseniella lebrijana*.

### 2. Molecular Ecology, Adaptation & Biogeography
We apply genetic and ecological tools to understand species boundaries, distribution patterns, and how organisms adapt across contrasting climatic zones.
* **Thermal Tolerance & Climate Resilience:** Evaluating the larval physiological adaptation, distribution mechanisms, and thermal thresholds of marine organisms like *Pollicipes elegans*.
* **Genetic Diversity & Marker Development:** Engineering genome-derived microsatellite and molecular markers for a broad range of flora and fauna to clarify population structures.

### 3. Biodiversity Conservation & Multi-Omics
We combine rigorous fieldwork with molecular diagnostics and computational tools to document and preserve vulnerable ecosystems.
* **Ichthyology & Computer Vision:** Analyzing the morphometric diversity and community structures of freshwater fish in areas like the Magdalena Medio using deep learning on field photography.
* **Evolutionary Systems:** Developing educational tools and researching developmental molecular traits, ranging from cave fauna evolution to vitellogenin conservation.

---

## Computational Pipelines & Toolsets
Our lab heavily utilizes bioinformatics workflows to transform high-throughput sequencing data into biological insights. We build, maintain, and implement reproducible workflows focusing on:
* **Long-Read & Short-Read Sequencing:** Processing and assembly pipelines optimized for technologies like Oxford Nanopore Sequencing.
* **Phylogenomics & Population Structure:** Scripting and pipeline development using Nextflow, R, and Python.
* **Phylogenetic Inferences:** Extensive deployment of core evolutionary toolkits including MrBayes, IQ-TREE, FastANI, and ASTRAL-W.
